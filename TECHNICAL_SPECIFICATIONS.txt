1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SYSTEM OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Battery Thermal Management System (BTMS) is an enterprise-grade, AI-driven 
microservices platform engineered to monitor, simulate, and optimize the thermal 
performance of high-density lithium-ion battery packs for electric vehicles (EVs) 
and grid storage systems.

Physical Target Specification:
- Battery Cells: 10 x 21700 Cylindrical Cells
- Chemistry: Lithium-ion (NMC/NCA)
- Enclosure: Aluminum Alloy Casing
- Microchannel Geometry: Embedded Circular Channels
- Hydraulic Diameter (Dh): 1.0 mm
- Channel Length (L): 0.1 m (100 mm)
- Target Safe Operating Range: 15.0°C – 45.0°C
- Temperature Uniformity (ΔT): < 5.0°C across all cells
- Coolant Medium: Al2O3 / Deionized Water NF

3. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & MICROSERVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Sensing & Simulation Layer: Generates 1 Hz telemetry for 10 x 21700 cells.
- Ingestion Layer: Flask service parsing POST /telemetry with Min-Max normalization.
- Broker Layer: Apache Kafka message broker; ingestion produces to and AI Engine consumes from the battery_stream topic.
- AI Inference & CFD Layer: FastAPI service running the IsolationForest anomaly detector and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) physics calculations.
- Authentication Layer: FastAPI service for User RBAC handling JWT tokens and bcrypt password hashing.
- Presentation Layer: React/Three.js digital twin rendering 3D thermal gradients and analytics.

4. MACHINE LEARNING & AI LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Model: Unsupervised Isolation Forest (scikit-learn)
Purpose: Detect pre-runaway signatures (voltage sags, current spikes, thermal divergence) and assign anomaly severity without labeled historical failure data.

Model Mathematics:
- Uses an ensemble of 200 isolation trees (iTrees).
- Evaluates average path length h(x).
- Anomaly Score s(x, n) = 2^(-E(h(x)) / c(n)).
  - s -> 1.0 (Strong Anomaly)
  - s -> 0.5 (Borderline/Warning)
  - s -> 0.0 (Normal)

Feature Vector (8-Dimensional):
1. Cell surface temperature (°C)
2. Terminal voltage (V)
3. Discharge/Charge current (A)
4. State of Charge (%)
5. Normalized temperature (T_i - T_min) / (T_max - T_min)
6. Pack mean temperature (Avg of 10 cells)
7. Pack thermal spread (Max - Min temperature)
8. Cell deviation from pack mean (°C)

Online Learning & Retraining Lifecycle:
- Cold-Start Bootstrap: Generates 800 synthetic safe-bound samples for immediate model readiness at Tick 0.
- Rolling Sample Buffer: Thread-safe deque (maxlen=1000) of live operating samples.
- Non-Blocking Retraining: Re-fits StandardScaler and IsolationForest every 50 ticks to learn pack-specific aging, seasonal ambient temp, and internal impedance drifts.

5. COMPUTATIONAL FLUID DYNAMICS (CFD) THERMAL OPTIMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The CFD engine models forced-convection laminar nanofluid flow through circular microchannels embedded within the battery pack aluminum cold plate.

Coolant: Al2O3 (aluminum oxide) nanoparticle suspension in deionized water.
Volume Fraction (φ): 0.5% - 3.0% (0.005 - 0.030)

Thermophysical Property Models:
- Effective Density (ρ_nf): Derived from mass-weighted volume mixture of Al2O3 and Water.
- Effective Dynamic Viscosity (μ_nf): Einstein Viscosity Model.
- Effective Thermal Conductivity (k_nf): Maxwell Dilute Suspension Model.

Flow & Heat Transfer Correlations:
- Flow Velocity (v): Computed via Reynolds Number Inversion: v = (Re * μ_nf) / (ρ_nf * D_h).
- Nusselt Number (Nu): Graetz-Dittus-Boelter correlation for thermally developing laminar flow (Nu >= 3.66 limit).
- Convective Heat Transfer Coefficient (h): Derived via h = (Nu * k_nf) / D_h.

Dynamic Cooling Control Matrix (AI Coupled):
- Normal (T <= 40°C, Anomaly s < 0.5): 
  Target Re: 450 | Nanoparticle φ: 0.5% | Velocity: ~0.395 m/s | Heat Transfer: ~2450 W/m²K
- Elevated/Hotspot (Warning, 0.5 <= s < 0.8): 
  Target Re: 580 | Nanoparticle φ: 1.5% | Velocity: ~0.518 m/s | Heat Transfer: ~3120 W/m²K
- Critical/Spike (T > 45°C, Anomaly s >= 0.8): 
  Target Re: 680 | Nanoparticle φ: 3.0% | Velocity: ~0.621 m/s | Heat Transfer: ~3890 W/m²K

6. SECURITY & AUTHENTICATION SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- RBAC (Role-Based Access Control): Distinguishes Operator (read-only diagnostics) vs Admin (override setpoints).
- Passwords: Cryptographic salting and hashing using bcrypt (12 rounds).
- Tokens: Stateless HMAC-SHA256 JWT Bearer tokens.
- Protections: Anti-SQLi protections via parameterized SQLite3 execution. Defends against XSS with Pydantic field validators. Rejects none-algorithm tokens and detects signature tampering.
